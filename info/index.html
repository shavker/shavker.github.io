Anton Shavkero is an advertising and creative director at the CLAN design team. His portfolio features projects for brands like PUMA, ASTON MARTIN and NIKE. Many of his works have earned international recognition, receiving awards at festivals such as Berlin Commercial, Berlin Music Video Awards, Ciclope, and Offf, as well as being featured in professional web magazines like Stash Media and EyeCandy.
