Anton Shavkero is a director, creative director, and artist working across film, design, CG, and emerging technology. His practice moves between commercial commissions and visual experiments, including projects for NIKE, ASTON MARTIN and RAY-BAN. He is interested in using new tools to build images and worlds that feel unfamiliar while keeping idea, emotion, and visual language at the center.
