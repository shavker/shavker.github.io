Anton ShavkeroDirector / Creative Director / ArtistDeal Memo

Anton Shavkero is a director, creative director and artist.His territory: experiments with images and technology, creating visual solutions that didn’t exist yesterday.

Deal MemoDownload PDF

He freely mixes live action, 3D, AI and new tools, constantly looking for new ways to work with images, storytelling and reality itself. Technology is not the goal for him, but a tool for finding unexpected visual solutions and making the creative process itself more interesting.

Anton is a co-founder of the multidisciplinary design team CLAN and the founder of AI label No Title Yet. His work ranges from independent and cultural projects to large commercial collaborations. The boundaries between them are often intentionally blurry.

He has worked with Nike, Puma, Aston Martin, PlayStation, Ray-Ban and Sphere Las Vegas, and collaborated with artists including A$AP Rocky and Deadmau5.

Every project is a chance to experiment, break a few rules and make something he hasn't seen before.
