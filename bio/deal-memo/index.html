Fee

€15,000 per shift. Payment within 14 calendar days from the start of the Director's work.

  • A shift is a full working day of creative direction and may include pre-production, live-action production, or CG/post-production supervision.
  • One shift includes up to 16 working hours. Additional hours are charged as a full additional shift unless agreed otherwise.
  • For larger projects (3+ shifts) or long-term involvement, a discount can be discussed.
  • 50% advance payment of the total fee is required before the Director starts work.
Cancellation

Live action: cancellation more than 72 hours before the scheduled start: 50% of the fee. Less than 72 hours before the shoot: 100%.

Full / partial CG: if cancelled after confirmation and work has started, 50% of the fee based on planned shifts. Completed shifts are paid at 100%.

Shoot transfer

Transfer within 4 calendar days with no conflicting confirmed commitment: fee unchanged. If rescheduling conflicts with another confirmed project, that project takes priority. Transfer over 4 calendar days: additional 50% of one shift fee.

Travel
  • 4–5★ hotel, if available.
  • Round-trip tickets fully covered.
  • Economy up to 5 hours; Premium Economy / Business over 5 hours (Star Alliance).
  • Night flights between 00:00 and 08:00 must be Business class.
  • Per diem: €200/day for international travel.
  • Local SIM card with internet access.
Director approval

By selecting Anton Shavkero as Director, the Client confirms acceptance of the creative vision outlined in the treatment and agrees to support its execution unless otherwise agreed in advance.

Director’s edit & portfolio

After delivery of the Client’s version, the Director may produce their own cut within two weeks using reasonable post-production resources. The Director has the right to use their cut, intermediate versions, and project materials for portfolio and self-promotion. This condition is mandatory and must be included in the final contract.
